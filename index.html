Till & Floor Guide

Starting Your Shift: Settings → Rota & OCR Upload → Duty Rota. Select Staff ID → Clock In.


Finding a Product: Open the GS1 scanner or Overview search. Scan or type GTIN / RMS / Alshaya. Auto-decodes (01), (17), (10). Shows Rack · Shelf · Facing + photo, looked up against the master product DB.


Completing a Sale: Shelf & Rebalance → FEFO POS. Scan items → cart. The earliest-expiring batch is picked automatically. Card / Cash / Other → Complete Sale & Generate Receipt.


Recording Daily KPIs: KPI Analytics → Daily Agenda. Enter Sale, TRN, No7, Aura per slot. ATV, No7 % and Aura % auto-calc. Import with the SAMPLE_KPI_DAILY.csv format.


Adding Staff: Settings → Staff & Incentive Setup → + Add Staff, or import SAMPLE_STAFF.csv.


Adding Images: Settings → Images, or drag onto a shelf slot in the Planogram.


Uploading Master Product List: Settings → Store & Target → Upload Master Product List → CSV with RMS Code,Barcode,Description.
